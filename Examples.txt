Example 1 - Opening

Starting Board:
xxBBWxxxxWxxxxxxxxxxxxx

Original Move:
Place White at position 0.

Improved Move:
Place White at position 8.

Why We Prefer the Improved Move:
The improved move creates a two piece mill opportunity at positions 8, 9, and 10. The original function only considers piece difference, so it doesn't see this future opportunity to complete a mill.


Example 2 - Midgame/Endgame

Starting Board:
xxxxWBxWWxWBBxxBWxxWxWB

Original Move:
Move White from position 10 to position 14.

Improved Move:
Move White from position 4 to position 3.

Why We Prefer the Improved Move:
The improved move gives White many more available moves while also keeping a future mill threat. The original function mainly looks at piece difference and reducing Black's available moves, so it does not see the increased mobility for White.
